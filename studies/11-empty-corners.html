The two empty corners: what goes under U3 and under U5?

Two corners of the wide layout read as gaps rather than room. In Projects, it's the space right of U3's two lamps and above Step by Step Strength. In Research, it's the space under U5 and above Quantifier Variance. Everything else in both sections stays as it is on the site. The only thing that changes is what fills each corner.

Each corner gets the same four candidates, each fitted to its corner and fed from a chip pad that's free today: a pin header at the end of a bus, a second chip with two capacitors and a staggered tail, a serpentine (a length-matched bus that meanders), and a crystal with its two load capacitors, the part that sits beside every microcontroller. Each option shows its section from the top, cut off a few lines under the corner, with the corner as it is now first for comparison.

The panel at the bottom draws both sections in full, with any filler in each corner. The new designators here (J2, J3, U9, U10, Y1, Y2, C1 to C4) are only for the study. The one you pick is renumbered into the sequence when it's built.
