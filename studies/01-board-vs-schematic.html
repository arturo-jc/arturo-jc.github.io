Study 01

Physical board or schematic: which vocabulary draws the circuit?

Five renderings of one circuit, from pure board to pure schematic. Every wire, part and terminal sits in the same place in all five; only the drawing idiom changes. Background, fonts, text column and density are held fixed.

Detail and density come later. Nothing here is skeuomorphic, and the amount of circuitry is one middling guess.

In each recipe: pale = taken from the board · orange = taken from the schematic
