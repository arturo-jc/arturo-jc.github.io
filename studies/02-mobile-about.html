About on a phone: where does the circuit go when there is no room beside the copy?

Four versions of the About section at 390 px wide. The copy, the type, the palette, the trace weight and the pitch are the same in all four, and the same as on the desktop page: one drawing unit is about one pixel here, so body text is 16.8 px. The orange net is also fixed: it leaves U1 and runs in along the heading's own row.

What changes is how much circuit there is, and where: above the copy, below it, and down its left side. Each option is built from three switches, and the bench at the bottom lets you set them yourself. The dashed tick on each one's right edge marks the fold: roughly where a phone's first screen ends.
