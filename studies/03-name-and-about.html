Study 03

The name and About, side by side: where does the circuit go?

Three versions of the top of the page at desktop width, with the name as the page's heading on the left and the new copy on the right. The copy, its type, the palette, the pitch and the trace weight are the same in all three, and the same as on the site. So is the orange net's job: it ends on a via just past Arturo, level with the middle of its capitals.

What changes is what the circuit does with two blocks instead of one: runs a bus between them, cradles them as a pair, or sets them on a diagonal. Each drawing is made for the way the name wraps in it.
