Study 05

Research on a wide screen: one column, a grid, or two staggered columns?

Three ways of arranging the four papers at desktop width. The heading, the papers, their type, the column width, the palette, the pitch and the trace weight are the same in all three. A paper is its title, which links to the published article, its journal, and its summary. The titles are long, so they wrap onto two lines.

Above each option is the foot of Projects, changed the same way in all three: a bus leaves U4 to the east and drops off the bottom of the board beside Step by Step Strength, and Research picks it up at its top edge in the same columns. So the join is held fixed; what changes is where the four papers sit, and so where the circuit finds its room once the bus arrives.

As in Projects, the orange net ends on a via beside each paper's title, level with the middle of its first line's capitals: just past the first line where it comes from the right, just before the title where it comes from the left.
