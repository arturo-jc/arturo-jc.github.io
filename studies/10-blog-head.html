The blog, with circuit only at the head

The two blog pages again, built from your four posts. This time the circuit stays round the top of each page and the text under it is plain: one column, 36 cells wide on a wide screen and 24 on a phone, in the same place in every option. Nothing runs down beside it.

What every option keeps: on the index, a chip and the off-page flag A0140 arriving at the heading, the same net that leaves About beside its "Blog". On the article, the same chip with an orange net to the title, and a flag in the top left pointing at "Blog", which links back to the index. What changes is where the rest of the head's circuit goes: A drops it down the margin beside the head and stops it short, B runs a bus across the top from a connector on the edge, and C turns it under the head and stops it short.

Each option shows the index, then the DevOps post (the longest title) cut off after its second section. The panel at the bottom draws any post in full, and it can add a via before each heading in the text, with no trace to it.
