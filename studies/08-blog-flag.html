Study 08

A link to the blog in About: an off-page flag

About as it is on the site, with one addition: a link to the blog, the word Blog set like a project's name, at the end of a net that leaves the board through a schematic off-page flag, the symbol a schematic draws where a net carries on onto another sheet. On a wide screen it sits in the empty pocket under U2's lamps, left of the copy; on a phone, under the copy on the right, where About's rail turns down into Projects.

What changes is where the net comes from and how the flag meets the word. Option A takes a new pad on U2's foot and puts the flag before the word; B instead carries the lamps' common return on past its via, the same flag; C is A with the word inside the flag, so the flag is the link's outline. The flag is outlined in orange on a blue trace in all three, so it draws the eye without a second orange net. Below each, the top of Projects, so the gap above its heading is in view.

The panel at the bottom mixes the three, and adds what the options leave out: no flag at all, only a via before the word, as each project's name has, and two other colourings, all orange and all blue.
