Study 06

The footer: what do the contacts plug into?

Four footers, each a board of its own under Research. The content is the same in all four: three contacts, each a name that is the link and a handle under it, set like a project's name and stack, and a copyright line set like silkscreen. The type, the palette, the pitch and the trace weight are the same too.

What changes is what the circuit does at each contact, and so how the contacts are arranged. Two options stack them in a column and two set them in a row, so each pair is a like-for-like comparison: a chip fanning out to vias against a rail of lit lamps, and schematic off-page flags against an edge connector along the bottom of the page.

Above each option is the foot of Research as it is on the site. Every option has a wide drawing and, below it, a narrow one at 390px. The narrow ones all run their rail down the right, where Research's runs down the left.
