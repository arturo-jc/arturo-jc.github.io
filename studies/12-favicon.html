A favicon drawn from the board

Three parts from the drawing, each cut out of the board on a rounded square of its ground, with the trace running off the crop as it would on the page. Everything else is held fixed: the palette, the trace weight and the size of the part in the frame.

Each is shown large, at the sizes a browser asks for (16 for a tab, 32 for a high-density tab, 48 and 180 for bookmarks and home screens), at 16 real pixels magnified, which is what a tab on a 1x screen gets, and in light and dark tab strips. “Try in this tab” makes it this page's own favicon.
