Study 04

Projects on a wide screen: side by side, in rows, or in one column?

Three versions of the Projects section at desktop width. The heading, the two projects, their type, the column width, the palette, the pitch and the trace weight are the same in all three. The heading is set at the size the About heading had; a project is its name, its stack, and its paragraph, and the name is the link to the repo.

The orange net's job is the same in all three as well: it is one net, and it ends on a via beside each project's name, level with the middle of its capitals. What changes is where the two projects sit, and so where the circuit finds its room.
