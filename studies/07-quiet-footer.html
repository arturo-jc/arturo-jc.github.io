Study 07

A quiet footer: how are the contacts arranged?

Study 06 gave the contacts a circuit of their own. This one goes the other way, toward the original portfolio's footer: a band in a colour of its own, edge to edge under the last board, with no drawing in it. Research's circuit ends above it, and the board ends where the band starts.

The band is held fixed at a deep blue, darker than the board, in every option. What changes is how the contacts are arranged inside it, and whether each is an icon, a word or a name with its handle. The type is the site's: names set like a project's, with the orange underline every link has; handles and the copyright in mono, like a stack or a journal. The contents sit on the boards' grid, across Research's two columns.

Above each option is the foot of Research as it is on the site. Each has a wide footer and a 390px one beside a note. At the bottom, a panel to mix your own, which also offers two other tones for the band.
