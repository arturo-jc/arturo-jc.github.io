The 404 page: what has gone wrong on the board?

The old site's 404 is a big "404", "Page not found" and "The requested page could not be found." Every option here keeps that copy and adds a way home, set like a post's way back to the blog and behind an orange off-page flag, the net that leaves this page. The page is one board, like a blog page's head, then the footer.

What changes is the joke the board tells, one per option: a trace cut in two, a chip that was never fitted, a lamp that has gone out, and a display that reads 404, which moves the number out of the type and into the drawing.

Each option is shown wide, then at 390px beside a note. At the bottom, a panel mixes any drawing with either heading and either wording.
