Study 14

The image a link to the site shows

Three crops of the site's own drawing, each 1200 × 630, the size LinkedIn, Slack, Discord and X all ask for. They are screenshots of the home page with nothing redrawn: the board at 1284 wide and twice the pixels, fully drawn, cropped and scaled down. What differs is only the crop and which type is shown.

Each is shown in a LinkedIn post, where it is about 550 pixels wide with the title and the domain under it, in a Discord embed, smaller, under the title and the description, and at full size. The title and the description in the mocks are the ones the site now sends.
